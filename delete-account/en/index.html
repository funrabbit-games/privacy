Account and Data Deletion

TakeDoll · Developer: FunRabbit

한국어 English

This page explains how to delete your TakeDoll (the "Game") account and the data stored with it.

Option 1. Delete in the Game (immediate)

  1. Open the Game and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice when prompted.
  4. When deletion is complete, the Game restarts from the beginning.

Option 2. Request by email (if you uninstalled the Game or cannot open it)

Send an email with the following details.

  • To: funrabbit.support@gmail.com
  • Subject: TakeDoll Account Deletion Request
  • Body: the Google account email address you used to sign in to the Game

After verifying your identity, we will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you only played as a guest, your account is tied to your device and we cannot verify you by email. Please use Settings → Delete Account in the Game.

Data that is deleted

DataHandling
Account information (account identifier UID, Google account identifier and email)Deleted immediately
Game data (progress, in-game currency and items, settings)Deleted immediately
Game data stored on your deviceDeleted immediately

Data that may be retained

DataReason and period
In-app purchase historyKept by Google Play as payment records, under Google's policies and applicable law
Analytics data (usage statistics not linked to your account)Automatically deleted after the Google Analytics retention period (up to 14 months)
Advertising dataHandled under the retention policies of the ad services (Unity Ads / LevelPlay)
Deleted data cannot be recovered, and purchased currency and items will also be lost.
